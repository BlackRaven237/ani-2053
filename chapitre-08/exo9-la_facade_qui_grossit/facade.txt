Les autres : For the remaining ones,
- SetView(), GetView(), GetDefaultView(), ResetView(), IsViewCustom(), SetViewport(), GetViewport() are used
to Manage views(Camera)
- SetResizePolicy(), GetResizePolicy(), GetDesignSize() are Resize policies
- GetSize() is used to access our Target size in pixels:
- MapCoordsToPixel(), MapPixelToCoords() are mapping relations from pixels to coords and vice-versa
- GetRenderer(), GetRenderer() are used to access our native renderer
- Capture() for screenshots

void Draw(const NkIDrawable2D &drawable) is the only method said to be temporary and will be removed

Le rapport : we obtain a ratio of 7:17. Yes, This Facade pattern effectively keeps its promise because
in a real project i may have to use more of it's public methods like those to manage the views
or that to access the native renderer.
