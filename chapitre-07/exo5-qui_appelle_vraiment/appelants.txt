Les différences :
As first difference, we can notice the size of both output. By text, has up to 800 lines while By Stack has only 6.
This can be explained by the fact that by text, we searched all possibles occurences of the word 'Draw' in 'include/'

Another difference is precision. By text, shows us as bunch of occurences with some not even related to what we're 
working on while by Stack, shows us all function calls leading to function we are actually searching for.

Nevertheless, By Text has a small advantage over By stack. By text, we can see the entire function signatures which is
beginner-friendly while By Stack is a little bit more technical and thus requires some level of experience especially 
with debuggers
